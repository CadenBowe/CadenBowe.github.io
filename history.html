The History of Motorcycles

Discover the journey of motorcycles, from their humble beginnings to the powerful machines we know today.

Milestones in Motorcycle History

Year Event Details
1885 First Motorcycle The Daimler Reitwagen, built by Gottlieb Daimler and Wilhelm Maybach, is considered the first motorcycle.
1901 First Production Motorcycle Indian Motorcycle Company produces the first commercially successful motorcycles.
1923 BMW R32 BMW introduces the R32, the first motorcycle with a boxer engine and shaft drive.
1959 Honda Enters the Market Honda releases the C100 Super Cub, becoming one of the best-selling motorcycles of all time.
1990 Advent of Superbikes Motorcycles like the Honda CBR900RR redefine the performance bike category.
